# Kestrel Couriers — Rate Card 2025

This rate card sets out the prices that Kestrel Couriers Ltd. charges Bluefin Logistics GmbH for last-mile parcel delivery in Germany from 1 January 2025 until 31 December 2025.

## Delivery Zones

Zone A covers the city areas of Hamburg and Berlin. Zone B covers all other German cities with more than 100,000 inhabitants. Zone C covers the rest of mainland Germany. Deliveries to the North Sea and Baltic islands are not offered.

## Prices

A standard parcel of up to 5 kg costs EUR 4.20 in Zone A, EUR 5.10 in Zone B and EUR 6.40 in Zone C. Each additional kilogram costs EUR 0.35. Same-day delivery is available in Zone A only, for a surcharge of EUR 3.00 per parcel.

## Fuel Surcharge

A fuel surcharge of 12% is added to all prices in this rate card. The surcharge is reviewed every quarter against the German diesel price index and changes are announced two weeks in advance.

## Claims

Claims for lost or damaged parcels must be submitted within fourteen (14) days of the scheduled delivery date, together with photos of the packaging and of the content. Compensation is limited to EUR 500 per parcel unless additional insurance was purchased.

## Invoicing

Kestrel invoices Bluefin weekly, every Monday, for the deliveries of the previous week. Invoices are payable within twenty-one (21) days of the invoice date. Disputed items must be reported within seven days.

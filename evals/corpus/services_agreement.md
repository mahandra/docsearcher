# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on 3 March 2025 between Northwind Analytics Ltd., a company registered in England and Wales under number 08812345 (the "Provider"), and Bluefin Logistics GmbH, a company registered in Hamburg, Germany (the "Customer").

## 1. Definitions

"Services" means the data analytics platform, the related hosting and the support services described in Schedule A. "Service Credits" means the amounts credited to the Customer under clause 6 when the Provider fails to meet the availability commitment. "Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement, whether oral, written or electronic. "Business Day" means a day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a public holiday in England.

## 2. Term

This Agreement starts on the Effective Date of 1 April 2025 and continues for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months. After the initial term, the Agreement renews automatically for successive renewal periods of twelve (12) months each, unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3. Fees and Payment

3.1 The Customer shall pay a monthly subscription fee of EUR 18,500 for the Services. Professional services ordered separately are charged at a daily rate of EUR 1,200 per consultant.

3.2 The Provider invoices the subscription fee monthly in advance. Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. All amounts are exclusive of VAT, which the Customer shall pay at the applicable rate.

3.3 If the Customer fails to pay an undisputed invoice on time, the Provider may charge interest on the overdue amount at a rate of 1.5% per month from the due date until the date of payment.

3.4 The Provider may increase the fees once per calendar year by giving at least sixty (60) days written notice. Any single increase shall not exceed 5% of the fees in effect before the increase.

3.5 If the Customer disputes an invoice in good faith, it shall notify the Provider in writing within fifteen (15) days of receiving it, stating the reasons. The parties shall try to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days; the undisputed part of the invoice remains payable on time.

## 4. Termination

4.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other party at least sixty (60) days written notice. Termination for convenience during the initial term does not release the Customer from paying the subscription fees for the remainder of the initial term.

4.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other party:

4.2.1 commits a material breach of this Agreement and, where the breach is capable of remedy, fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing the breach;

4.2.2 becomes insolvent, enters into liquidation or administration, or makes an arrangement with its creditors;

4.2.3 is subject to a change of control in favour of a direct competitor of the terminating party.

4.3 Upon termination for any reason, the Provider shall make the Customer Data available for export in CSV and JSON formats for a period of forty-five (45) days. After that period, the Provider shall delete the Customer Data from its production systems and backups within a further thirty (30) days, and confirm the deletion in writing on request.

4.4 Clauses 5 (Confidentiality), 7 (Limitation of Liability) and 9 (Governing Law) survive the termination of this Agreement.

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secret and use it only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement. Disclosure is permitted to employees, contractors and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as strict as these.

5.2 The confidentiality obligations continue for five (5) years after the termination of this Agreement. Trade secrets remain protected for as long as they remain trade secrets.

5.3 The obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving party, or that the receiving party must disclose by law, provided that it gives the disclosing party prompt notice where legally allowed.

## 6. Service Levels

6.1 The Provider shall make the production platform available 99.9% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance. Scheduled maintenance takes place on Sundays between 02:00 and 06:00 CET and is announced at least five (5) Business Days in advance.

6.2 If monthly availability falls below 99.9%, the Customer is entitled to a Service Credit of 5% of the monthly fee. If availability falls below 99.0%, the Service Credit is 15% of the monthly fee. If availability falls below 95.0%, the Service Credit is 30% of the monthly fee. Service Credits are capped at 30% of the monthly fee for any month.

6.3 Service Credits must be claimed in writing within thirty (30) days after the end of the affected month. Service Credits are the Customer's sole financial remedy for availability failures, without prejudice to the right to terminate under clause 4.2.1.

6.4 Support requests are classified by severity. For Severity 1 incidents (the platform is unavailable or unusable for all users), the Provider responds within one (1) hour, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. For Severity 2 incidents (a major function is impaired), the Provider responds within four (4) business hours. For all other requests the Provider responds within two (2) Business Days.

## 7. Limitation of Liability

7.1 Neither party is liable for any indirect or consequential loss, loss of profit, loss of revenue or loss of goodwill, even if it was advised of the possibility of such loss.

7.2 Each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection with this Agreement in any contract year is limited to an amount equal to the fees paid and payable by the Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the event that gave rise to the claim.

7.3 The limitations in this clause do not apply to liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, for breach of clause 5 (Confidentiality), or for the Customer's obligation to pay the fees.

## 8. Data Protection

8.1 The Provider processes personal data on behalf of the Customer as a processor, in accordance with the Data Processing Addendum in Schedule B. The Customer Data is hosted in data centres located in Frankfurt, Germany, and is not transferred outside the European Economic Area without the Customer's prior written consent.

8.2 The Provider shall notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Customer Data. The notice shall describe the nature of the breach, the categories of data concerned and the measures taken.

8.3 The Provider may engage sub-processors with the Customer's prior consent. The current list of sub-processors is set out in Schedule C. The Provider shall inform the Customer of any intended change at least thirty (30) days in advance, and the Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds.

## 9. Governing Law and Disputes

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.

9.2 The parties shall first try to resolve any dispute through negotiation between their senior executives within twenty (20) Business Days. A dispute that is not resolved by negotiation shall be finally settled by arbitration under the LCIA Rules, by a sole arbitrator, with the seat of arbitration in London. The language of the arbitration is English.

## 10. Miscellaneous

10.1 All notices under this Agreement must be in writing and sent by e-mail with a copy by registered post to the addresses given in Schedule D. A notice sent by e-mail is deemed received on the next Business Day.

10.2 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, which may not be unreasonably withheld. The Provider may assign this Agreement to an affiliate upon written notice.

10.3 Neither party is liable for a delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control, such as natural disasters, war or large-scale power outages. If such an event continues for more than sixty (60) days, either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice.
